-Note on The White Death
Weißer Geist (The White Death)

- **What It Is:** A terrifying, zombie-like affliction on Grand Ile that attacks a Gyun's **soul and memories** rather than just their physical body.
- **How It Affects Victims:** As the disease progresses, it **strips away the victim's soul and identity**, leaving their trapped consciousness inside a hollowed-out shell (turning them into a "Hollow" or zombified husk).
- **Origin & Lore Connection:** Linked directly to **Xiolem**—specifically tied to the events after Xiolem made land in a forbidden or dangerous area where a _Plakett_ revealed forbidden knowledge ("told them too much").
- **Rumor vs. Reality:** While many across Grand Ile dismiss it as a dark rumor or superstition, the threat is very real and actively spreading.
