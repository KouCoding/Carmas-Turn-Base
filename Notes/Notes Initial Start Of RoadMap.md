Tribes:
Black Hey: underneath the sand of Emizadi a country of a barren desert these Gyuns rarely shown themselves at day, unless to photosynthesize as if they were a plant, they do however come out at night to hunt for food and patrols. 
Emizadi are Rich in rare crafting materials as well gems and keeping a well heated rivalry with Xiolem. They also are one of the three most largest population. (Kaaljin / Kaalujin as currency.) 

Anssepu: Is another tribe that's vastly populated, shares the same coin as Emizadi but prefer to get by using offerings and doing favors in substitute of money. They are very rooted in community being together and tribal bonds.

DoKalakee: Another tribe with a population to match the first two mentioned making up Grand Ile entirety.  Tribe thrives no matter the seasons, atmosphere, and unpredictable gravity.

Xiolem: Main rival to Emizadi, and uses stolen currency (Ulexo & Yuuk) that's from a different territory. This tribe has 3 leaders **Ppilokghou (Head)**, **N'nicewydo (Body)**, and Qqoiluaaof (Soul)**.
